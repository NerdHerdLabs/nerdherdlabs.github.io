Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy covers Footprints and other apps by NerdHerd Labs.

Your location stays on your device

Footprints uses your iPhone's location services to build a private journal of the places you visit. Your location history, places and journal entries are stored on your device. We do not collect, sell or share them, and we do not use them for advertising.

What we don't do

No ads, no third-party tracking, no selling of data.

Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it. Sharing your location and sharing your memories are separate choices, and you can turn either off at any time.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its data from your device.

Contact

Questions? Email hello@nerdherdlabs.com.

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
